📘PRESENT SIMPLE
Present Simple
+ Subject + verb(s) · − Subject + don’t/doesn’t + verb · ? Do/Does + subject + verb?
She plays guitar. / She doesn’t play piano. / Does she play?
⚡PRESENT CONTINUOUS
Present Continuous
+ am/is/are + verb-ing · − am/is/are + not + verb-ing · ? Am/Is/Are + subject + verb-ing?
She is making an album now. / Is she recording?

🥑THERE IS / THERE ARE
There is / There are
Singular / uncountable: There is · Plural: There are
Questions: Is there...? / Are there...? · Negatives: There isn’t / There aren’t

Countable: apples, cookies, oranges. Uncountable: water, sugar, bread, oil.

How many + plural countable · How much + uncountable · some in affirmative, any often in questions/negative.
